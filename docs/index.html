About

FakeProt generates synthetic protein families by simulating evolution along a random phylogeny. Each branch evolves under the WAG substitution model with gamma-distributed site rates and probabilistic physicochemical constraints.

Outputs include an MSA, gene tree, species cladogram, ortholog group assignments, and a per-column physicochemical group report. FakeProt is primarily designed for educational purposes, to explore how sequence divergence, site-rate variation, and gene duplication shape protein families. Because the true phylogeny, ancestral sequences, and ortholog assignments are known, its outputs can also serve as ground-truth benchmarks for multiple sequence aligners, phylogeny reconstruction methods, ancestral sequence reconstruction methods, and ortholog detection tools.

Runs in-browser via Pyodide. No data leave your browser. First load downloads packages; subsequent loads use the browser cache.
